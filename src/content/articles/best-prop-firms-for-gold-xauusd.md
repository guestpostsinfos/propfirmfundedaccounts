Gold is one of the most popular instruments among prop traders and one of the most misjudged. XAUUSD behaves nothing like a forex major — it moves several times as much in dollar terms — and traders who carry major-pair habits onto it breach fast. Choosing a gold-friendly firm and sizing correctly are two halves of the same problem.

## What makes a firm good for gold

Beyond the universal must-haves (reliable payouts, fair drawdown), gold traders should weigh three things:

1. **XAUUSD spreads specifically.** Gold's spread varies more between firms than the majors do, and a wide one quietly eats tight targets. Check the actual gold spread, not the EUR/USD one.
2. **Forgiving, static drawdown.** Gold's large range needs room. A [tight trailing limit](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) that's manageable on forex is easily breached by one normal gold swing — favour wide static drawdown, the profile covered in [best low-drawdown prop firms](/best/best-low-drawdown-prop-firms/).
3. **News flexibility.** Gold reacts hard to inflation prints, rate decisions and risk sentiment, so a permissive [news-trading policy](/guides/prop-firm-news-trading-rules/) helps active gold traders.

Most established forex firms in the [best prop firms of 2026](/best/best-prop-firms/) offer XAUUSD; the differentiators are spread and room.

## The sizing mistake that breaches gold traders

This is where most gold accounts die. A position sized to risk a comfortable amount on EUR/USD, applied at the same lot size to XAUUSD, risks *multiples* more because gold moves multiples more per point. The result is a "normal" trade that breaches a [daily drawdown](/tools/drawdown-calculator/) on an ordinary gold candle.

The fix is gold-specific sizing: use a stop appropriate to XAUUSD's real range, then let the [position size calculator](/tools/position-size-calculator/) — which includes a gold preset — convert your fixed risk percentage into a correspondingly smaller lot size. On gold, undersizing relative to your forex habits isn't caution; it's the only sizing that keeps a volatile instrument inside the rules. The same fast-move discipline applies to [scalping gold](/best/best-prop-firms-for-scalping/), where slippage around news compounds the risk.

## Shortlist

1. From the [best prop firms](/best/best-prop-firms/), check **XAUUSD spreads** and confirm gold is offered on your platform.
2. Keep those with **wide static drawdown** and a **permissive news policy**.
3. Size every gold trade for gold's range, and confirm your worst case survives with the [drawdown calculator](/tools/drawdown-calculator/).

Gold rewards traders who respect its volatility with both firm choice and sizing — and punishes everyone who treats it like just another pair.
