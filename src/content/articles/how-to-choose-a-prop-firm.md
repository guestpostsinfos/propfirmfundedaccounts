Most traders choose a prop firm backwards — starting with the discount code and the profit split, the two things that matter least. This framework reverses that. Work through it in order and the right firm for *you* falls out of the process, not out of a banner ad.

## The 7 steps, in priority order

1. **Verify payout reliability.** Does the firm have a documented, multi-year record of paying traders? Firms that survived the [2024–2026 shakeout](/guides/are-prop-firms-legit/) proved this the hard way. This is the single heaviest factor in [our methodology](/methodology/) at 35%.
2. **Check the drawdown type.** Static drawdown is predictable; trailing drawdown — especially intraday — is the leading cause of funded-account failure. Read [drawdown rules explained](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) and prefer static unless you specifically want otherwise.
3. **Weight the fee by pass rate.** The sticker price is meaningless alone. A tight-ruled cheap firm can cost more to actually get funded than a fair, pricier one.
4. **Confirm rule fit.** Match the rulebook to your style (see step below).
5. **Compare the real split.** Now — and only now — weigh profit split, and only between firms already strong on 1–4.
6. **Scan for red flags.** Run the firm past the [red-flags checklist](/guides/prop-firm-red-flags/); a cluster disqualifies it.
7. **Confirm payout timing.** Know the minimum trading days and cycle before you buy — see [how long payouts take](/guides/how-long-do-prop-firm-payouts-take/).

## Match the firm to how you actually trade

Step 4 is where most mismatches hide. Before comparing firms, write down how you make money, then check the rules against it:

| If you… | Prioritise a firm with… |
|---------|-------------------------|
| Trade news / events | No news window, or a wide one |
| Hold overnight / weekend | Explicit weekend-holding permission |
| Concentrate profit on big days | No tight consistency cap |
| Scalp fast | No minimum-hold-time rule |
| Risk big on single trades | No tight per-trade cap |

A firm that's ideal for a steady intraday grinder can be actively hostile to a streaky news trader. There's no universally "best" firm — only the best fit for your edge.
