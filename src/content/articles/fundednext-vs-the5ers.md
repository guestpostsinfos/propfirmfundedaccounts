FundedNext and The5ers appeal to different traders wearing the same "I want good terms" label. FundedNext optimises for **value and flexibility today**; The5ers optimises for **compounding one account over months.** Neither is better in the abstract — they're built for different goals.

## Head to head

| Factor | FundedNext | The5ers |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Founded | 2022 | 2016 |
| Signature feature | Pays eval profit on fail | Level-based scaling program |
| Split | to 95% | to 100% (scaled) |
| Drawdown | 5% daily / 10% static | Structured / level rules |
| Rewards | Cost-efficiency, flexibility | Patient, low-risk consistency |
| Track record | Shorter (newer) | Longer (since 2016) |

## FundedNext: value and flexibility now

[FundedNext's](/reviews/fundednext-review/) pitch is immediate value. The **pay-on-fail feature** — a share of your evaluation profit paid even if you don't pass — lowers the true cost of an attempt more than any discount, and the split scales toward 95% on a flexible, no-time-limit evaluation with forgiving static drawdown. For a trader who wants strong terms without committing to a slow growth program, and who values a cushion against a failed attempt, FundedNext is the efficient choice. Its main caveat is its 2022 vintage — a [shorter track record](/guides/are-prop-firms-legit/) than the legacy tier.

## The5ers: compounding over months

[The5ers](/reviews/the5ers-review/) is the opposite temperament. Its level-based [scaling program](/guides/prop-firm-scaling-plans-explained/) is the entire product: trade low-risk and consistent, progress through tiers, and grow one account's balance and split toward 100% over time. It rewards patience structurally, and its since-2016 history gives it the longer proven record. The cost is speed — this is a firm for traders who measure progress in months, not payouts-per-week, and whose defining trait is disciplined consistency.

## The decision

- **Choose FundedNext** if you want value and flexibility now, a cushion against a failed attempt, and a high split without a slow grind.
- **Choose The5ers** if you're a patient, low-risk trader aiming to compound a single account toward a 100% split, and you value a longer track record.

Both are credible; the split ceiling and scaling depth favour The5ers for the long game, while cost-efficiency and flexibility favour FundedNext.
