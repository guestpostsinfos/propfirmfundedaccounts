Ask a hundred breached traders what ended their account and almost none will say "the profit target." They'll describe a rule they underestimated — a trailing floor that caught them in profit, a daily limit at 4:55pm, a consistency cap that triggered a panic trade. The target gets all the attention; these rules do all the damage. Here's the field guide to what actually breaches accounts.

## The rules ranked by lethality

**1. Trailing drawdown.** The number one killer. A [trailing floor](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) follows your equity peak upward, converting profits into risk — intraday versions include floating profit, so a winning trade that pulls back can breach you mid-position. Prefer static; if you must trade trailing, take end-of-day.

**2. The daily-loss reset boundary.** Traders get ended holding a floating loss into the daily reset, breaching a limit they thought they had room under. Know your exact reset time and calculation base.

**3. Consistency caps (indirectly).** A [consistency violation](/guides/prop-firm-consistency-rules-explained/) usually only *defers* a payout — but the panic it causes leads to oversized trades that breach a drawdown. The rule doesn't kill the account; the reaction to it does.

## The clauses traders never read

These aren't headline rules, which is exactly why they breach the unprepared:

| Clause | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Minimum hold time | Voids fast-[scalped](/guides/prop-firm-news-trading-rules/) profits, may reset the account |
| Hedging / arbitrage ban | Voids trades using [banned techniques](/guides/can-you-use-ea-on-prop-firms/) |
| News window | Restricts trading around high-impact events |
| Weekend holding | [Auto-closes or penalises](/guides/prop-firm-weekend-holding-rules/) positions left open |
| Inactivity clause | Can close a dormant account after a set period |

None of these are hidden — they're all in the terms. They breach traders purely because those traders never searched for them before funding.

## The survival routine

Every breach above is avoidable with the same short routine:

1. **Know your drawdown type and exact breach prices** before the first trade — the [drawdown calculator](/tools/drawdown-calculator/) gives you the numbers.
2. **Know your reset time, consistency cap, and clause list** — read the terms, not the marketing.
3. **Size so a normal losing streak can't reach your drawdown** — confirm with the [risk of ruin calculator](/tools/risk-of-ruin-calculator/).
4. **Set a personal daily stop and never force trades** to chase a target or a payout.

The pattern across every breached account is the same: it wasn't a trading failure, it was a rule-knowledge failure. Master the rules that bite — the ones on this page — and you remove almost every way a funded account actually dies. The [risk management](/guides/prop-firm-risk-management/) that keeps you inside them is the same discipline that eventually gets you [scaled and paid](/guides/prop-firm-scaling-plans-explained/).
