Apex and MyFundedFutures are both trailing-drawdown futures firms, which means this comparison is less about finding the one with "better" drawdown and more about track record, pricing and flexibility — because at both, *how you handle trailing* decides your outcome more than which logo you pick.

## Head to head

| Factor | Apex Trader Funding | MyFundedFutures |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Pricing | One-time (2026 model) | Per plan |
| Track record | Long, established | Newer (2023) |
| Drawdown | EOD or intraday trailing | EOD / intraday / fixed by plan |
| Daily loss limit | Trailing-based | None on any plan |
| Consistency | 50% rule (2026) | Varies by plan |
| Best for | Track record, one-time pricing | Flexibility, no daily ceiling |

## Apex: established, one-time pricing

[Apex](/reviews/apex-trader-funding-review/) is one of the larger, longer-running futures firms, and its March 2026 overhaul modernised it: one-time pricing, a choice between end-of-day and intraday trailing, the MAE rule removed, and a 50% consistency rule. The end-of-day option is the meaningful improvement — it makes Apex far more forgiving than its old trailing-only structure. If you value a proven history and prefer paying once, Apex is the natural pick; just take the end-of-day drawdown option.

## MyFundedFutures: flexible, no daily ceiling

[MyFundedFutures'](/reviews/myfundedfutures-review/) draw is choice and the absence of a daily loss limit across all five plans. That flexibility lets you match the account to your style, but it demands self-discipline — no daily ceiling means no forced stop — and its Rapid plan's intraday trailing is a trap to avoid in favour of Core, Pro or Flex.

## The decision — and the rule that matters more

- **Choose Apex** for the longer track record and one-time pricing, with the end-of-day drawdown option.
- **Choose MyFundedFutures** for plan flexibility and no daily loss limit, with a gentler end-of-day or fixed plan.

But the bigger lesson applies to both: [trailing drawdown](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) is what breaches futures accounts, so whichever you choose, take the end-of-day option and model it with the [drawdown calculator](/tools/drawdown-calculator/) before trading real size.
