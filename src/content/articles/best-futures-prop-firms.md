## 1. Topstep — the futures benchmark

Operating since 2012, Topstep has paid traders through every market cycle and the 2024–2026 shakeout that killed 80+ firms. The Consistency Score annoys streaky traders — one big day can't carry your Combine — but it manufactures exactly the habits that keep funded traders alive. The 2026 payout structure (**100% of your first $10K**) is the best early-money deal relative to risk in the category. Full analysis: [Topstep review](/reviews/topstep-review/).

## 2. Apex Trader Funding — best value, sharper edges

Apex's **March 1, 2026 overhaul** was the biggest structural change in futures funding: one-time evaluation payments (the monthly bleed is gone), a choice between end-of-day and intraday trailing drawdown, the MAE rule removed, and a 50% consistency rule at payout. You keep **100% of your first $25K**, and accounts scale to $300K with multi-account stacking.

The remaining hazard is the one that was always there: the trailing drawdown ratchets up behind your equity peak, and it remains the leading cause of funded-account death at Apex. Take the EOD option, trade micros until you've banked distance from the floor, and read our [trailing drawdown guide](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) first. ## 3. MyFundedFutures — the simplicity pick

MyFundedFutures wins traders on one axis: you can read the entire rule set in five minutes and there are no scoring systems layered on top. Splits and pricing sit mid-pack. It's the right choice if rule complexity itself is what breaches you — an underrated failure mode.

## What to watch in futures funding in 2026

**Regulation is coming here first.** The CFTC's posture through 2026 points toward registration or licensing requirements for firms offering futures-linked funding. That's a survivability filter: firms with real US corporate structure, visible leadership and clean books (Topstep is the clearest case) can register; anonymous offshore operations can't. Position accordingly — and as always after the [industry's 80-firm collapse](/guides/are-prop-firms-legit/), **withdraw every payout cycle**.

**Micro contracts are the meta.** Every firm above allows MES/MNQ/MGC. Starting funded accounts on micros until you've built payout cushion is the single highest-expectancy habit in futures funding.
