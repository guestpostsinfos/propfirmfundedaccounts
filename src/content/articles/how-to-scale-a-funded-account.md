Passing a challenge is a one-time event; scaling a funded account is a campaign. The traders who turn a prop account into real income don't do it with a heroic month — they do it by stringing together many unremarkable, profitable, *breach-free* ones. Here's the realistic path, and why risk management is the whole game.

## The mechanic: milestones, not big months

Scaling plans reward consistency by design. As [scaling plans explained](/guides/prop-firm-scaling-plans-explained/) covers, you advance by hitting a target profit percentage over a minimum period without breaching — and each milestone typically raises **both your capital and your split**. That two-axis compounding is what makes six figures reachable: a higher percentage of a larger balance, milestone after milestone.

You can see the math with the [scaling plan projection calculator](/tools/scaling-plan-projection-calculator/) — project a modest per-milestone return across several tiers and watch the cumulative take-home accelerate. But the projection assumes one thing: you never breach.

## Why risk management *is* the growth strategy

Here's the part aggressive traders miss. A single [drawdown breach](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) resets all scaling progress — and some firms reset the clock after a losing period too. So the trader who sizes up to scale faster is the one most likely to breach and start over, while the patient trader compounds steadily. The [risk management](/guides/prop-firm-risk-management/) that keeps you funded — small fixed risk, a personal daily stop, never forcing trades — is *identical* to the discipline that scales you. They're not separate skills.

This inverts the intuition: you scale faster by risking *less* per trade, because survival is the rate-limiting step, not per-trade size.

## The realistic timeline and mindset

- **Think in months, not weeks.** Milestones require minimum periods; there's no shortcutting the clock.
- **Ignore the firm's headline ceiling.** The $4M cap isn't your constraint — your breach-free streak is.
- **Let six figures be a result, not a target.** Chasing the number directly produces the oversizing that resets you. Trade the process; the number is the byproduct.

## Confirm the payoff

As your balance and split grow, so does the value of each payout — confirm your real take-home at each tier with the [profit split calculator](/tools/profit-split-calculator/). The account that reaches six figures isn't the one with the flashiest edge; it's the one whose trader treated every milestone as a risk-management exercise first and a profit target second. Protect the downside relentlessly, string the periods together, and the compounding does the rest.
