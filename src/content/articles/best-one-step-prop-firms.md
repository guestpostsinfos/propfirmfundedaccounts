One-step evaluations are marketed as the fast lane to funded, and they are faster — but "faster" and "easier" aren't the same thing. Firms rarely give away the reduced bar for free; they claw it back in the drawdown or the target. Here's how to find a one-step firm that's genuinely a good deal, not just a quick one.

## What one-step actually means

A one-step challenge funds you after a single phase: hit one profit target inside the [drawdown rules](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) and you're funded, skipping the second verification phase of a [two-step challenge](/guides/one-step-vs-two-step-challenges/). The speed is real. The catch is the offset:

| Route | Speed | Common offset |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| One-step | Fastest evaluation | Tighter drawdown or higher target |
| Two-step | Slower | Gentler per-phase rules |
| [Instant](/guides/what-is-instant-funding/) | No evaluation | Higher fee, tightest rules |

So a one-step firm with a tight trailing drawdown can be *harder* to pass than a two-step firm with fair static rules. Phase count is a starting point, not the answer.

## How to pick a one-step firm

1. **Check the drawdown type and target on the one-step version specifically.** Firms often attach tighter rules to their faster route — that's where the offset hides.
2. **Prefer static over trailing.** A fast route through an intraday-trailing account can breach before you reach the target.
3. **Keep reliability first.** Speed to funded at a firm that doesn't pay is no bargain — the phase count never outranks payout reliability and drawdown fairness from [how to choose a prop firm](/guides/how-to-choose-a-prop-firm/).

[FundingPips](/reviews/fundingpips-review/) is a strong low-cost one-step option with static drawdown; other one-step firms sit throughout the [best prop firms of 2026](/best/best-prop-firms/).

## Who one-step suits

- **Confident, consistent traders who value speed:** one-step is ideal — you clear a single target and start earning sooner.
- **Traders who want room:** a two-step's gentler per-phase drawdown may serve you better, even though it's slower.

Before you buy for speed, weight the fee by a realistic pass rate with the [true cost calculator](/tools/challenge-cost-calculator/) and pressure-test the target against your win rate with the [pass simulator](/tools/challenge-pass-simulator/). The best one-step firm is the one where the fast route is also a *fair* one — not just the one that gets you to a tighter cage quickest.
