## What changed through 2026

- **Consolidation.** The shakeout that closed 80–100 firms left a smaller field of proven survivors and a much higher bar for trust.
- **Regulation.** Regulators in several jurisdictions moved toward registration requirements, gradually raising the floor.
- **Rule trends.** Per-trade risk caps spread, and more firms moved to one-time pricing over subscriptions.

The net effect for traders: an unproven track record is a bigger red flag in 2027 than it was two years ago.
