Topstep has refreshed how it rewards its best-performing funded traders. In July 2026 the firm rebranded its "Live performance bonuses" as the **Topstep Octagon** — a monthly bonus for Live Funded traders that sits alongside, not inside, the normal payout process.

## What changed

According to [Topstep coverage](https://proptradingvibes.com/prop-firms/topstep) of the update (introduced around July 6, 2026), **Live Funded traders earn monthly performance bonuses based on net profit and loss for a calendar month, paid directly into the Live Funded Account and independent of payouts and balance expansion.** In plain terms: it's an extra reward for a strong month, dropped straight into your live account, separate from the withdrawals and scaling you already get.

The Octagon rides on top of Topstep's existing structure — including the **90% Live Funded profit split** for sign-ups after January 12, 2026 — rather than replacing any of it.

## What it means for traders

The key detail is the **calendar-month** basis. A bonus tied to full-month net P&L rewards *consistency across the month*, not a single outlier day — the same profile Topstep's [consistency mechanics](/guides/prop-firm-consistency-rules-explained/) already encourage. Traders who grind steady green months benefit most; a strong week undone by a bad one nets out.

As always with bonus programs, the exact percentages, caps and conditions are the firm's to set and adjust, so confirm the live terms in your dashboard before counting on a number.
