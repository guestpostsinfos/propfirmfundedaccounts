FundingPips became one of the fastest-growing prop firms by ruthlessly undercutting everyone on price. The question we bought a challenge to answer: is the discount funded by tighter rules that breach you faster? Partly, yes — and whether that trade is worth it depends entirely on your trading style.

## What do you actually get at this price?

FundingPips offers three routes to funding: a **1-step evaluation** (10% profit target), a **2-step evaluation** (10%, then 5%) and **instant funding** (no evaluation, lower split, tightest rules). Pricing starts near $129 — for context, that's less than a quarter of FTMO's $619 for the popular $100K 2-Step tier when comparing similar sizes.

The catch sits in the risk parameters:

| Parameter | FundingPips | Industry standard |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Daily drawdown | 3–5% | 5% |
| Max drawdown | 6–10% | 10% |
| Profit target (1-step) | 10% | 10% |
| Profit target (2-step) | 10% / 5% | 8–10% / 5% |
| Base profit split | 60–100% by program | 80% |

A 3% daily limit sounds survivable until you do the math: two 1.5% losers and you're done for the day — and one gap through a stop can end the account. If you trade news, hold overnight, or run wide stops, price the tighter drawdown into your decision. Our [drawdown guide](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) covers how to size around it.

## Trading and payout experience

Our test challenge (2-step, $50K) executed cleanly on majors; spreads were a touch wider than FTMO's during Asian session. The platform lineup is modern (no MT4 nostalgia here), and the dashboard's daily-loss tracker resets on server time — know your reset hour, because "daily" drawdown breaches at 4:59pm server time are the most common avoidable failure.

Payout processing on our funded account took 4 business days — acceptable, not benchmark-setting.

## Who is FundingPips for?

**Buy it if:** this is your first challenge and you want the cheapest realistic rehearsal; or you're a disciplined intraday trader whose max daily loss is naturally under 2%. A failed $129 attempt teaches the same lesson as a failed $619 one.

**Skip it if:** you need room to breathe on drawdown (go [FundedNext](/reviews/fundednext-review/)), or you want maximum payout certainty (pay the FTMO premium — see our [FTMO review](/reviews/ftmo-review/)).

## Verdict: 4.2/5

FundingPips does exactly what it advertises: minimum viable price for a credible funded-account path. The tight daily drawdown is the real cost, and it's invisible until it breaches you.
