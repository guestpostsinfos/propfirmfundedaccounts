The5ers and FTMO are both blue-chip names, both survived the industry shakeout, and both pay reliably. So this comparison isn't about trust — it's about *time horizon.* One firm is built to grow a single account over months; the other is built to fund and pay you on proven, flexible terms right now.

## Head to head

| Factor | The5ers | FTMO |
|--------|---------|------|
| Founded | 2016 | 2015 |
| Core model | Level-based scaling program | Evaluation (1-step / 2-step) |
| Rewards | Low-risk, consistent, patient trading | Passing a clear evaluation |
| Split | Up to 100% (scaled) | To 90% |
| Drawdown | Structured / level rules | Static (5% / 10%) |
| Best mindset | "I think in months" | "Fund me and pay me" |

## The5ers: growth as the whole product

[The5ers](/reviews/the5ers-review/) doesn't treat scaling as a bonus feature — it *is* the product. Its level/growth program moves you up through tiers as you trade consistently and at low risk, compounding your balance and split toward 100% over time. This structurally rewards the exact behaviour most firms only tolerate: patience, small risk, steady green. If you're the trader who compounds a single account over months rather than chasing fast payouts, The5ers is designed for you — see [how scaling plans work](/guides/prop-firm-scaling-plans-explained/) for the mechanics it's built on. The cost is speed: growth is deliberately slow, and aggressive or streaky styles chafe against it.

## FTMO: the proven all-rounder

[FTMO's](/reviews/ftmo-review/) strength is being the benchmark — the most documented payout record in the industry, predictable static 5%/10% drawdown, and a straightforward path to a split near 90% without grinding through a level system. It accommodates a wider range of styles within its clear rules, and it funds you faster than a scaling program that rewards patience above all. The 2026 [per-trade risk cap](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) on funded accounts is the one rule to check against a concentrated-risk strategy.

## The decision

- **Choose The5ers** if you trade low-risk and consistent, think in months, and want a firm that structurally rewards compounding one account toward a 100% split.
- **Choose FTMO** if you want the industry-standard reliability, faster and more flexible funding, and strong terms without a slow level grind.

This is a style-and-horizon choice between two firms that both clear the [reliability bar](/best/best-prop-firms/).
