Topstep and MyFundedFutures represent two eras of futures prop trading. Topstep is the decade-old benchmark built on structure and a long payout record; MyFundedFutures is the flexible newcomer built on choice and no daily ceiling. Your pick depends on whether you want proven guardrails or tunable freedom.

## Head to head

| Factor | Topstep | MyFundedFutures |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Founded | 2012 | 2023 |
| Track record | Long, proven | Newer |
| Daily loss limit | Structured loss limit | None on any plan |
| Consistency | Consistency Score | Varies by plan |
| Plans | Standardised | Five types |
| Best for | Reliability, structure | Flexibility, no daily ceiling |

## Topstep: proven structure

[Topstep](/reviews/topstep-review/) has funded futures traders since 2012, which alone puts it ahead on the metric that matters most — a documented payout history through multiple cycles. Its structured loss limits and [Consistency Score](/guides/prop-firm-consistency-rules-explained/) impose discipline automatically, filtering out lottery-ticket trading and, in the process, rehearsing exactly the profile every firm wants to pay. The cost is flexibility: streaky styles chafe against the consistency requirement, and the account comes as-is rather than tuned to you.

## MyFundedFutures: flexible and no daily ceiling

[MyFundedFutures'](/reviews/myfundedfutures-review/) appeal is choice — five plan types and, crucially, **no daily loss limit on any of them**, so your only hard breach line is the account drawdown. That removes the classic end-of-session ceiling breach, but also removes the circuit-breaker that forces you to stop. Pair that with the intraday-trailing risk on its Rapid plan (choose Core, Pro or Flex to avoid it) and the picture is clear: excellent for a disciplined trader who wants room, riskier for one who needs guardrails.

## The decision

- **Choose Topstep** for the longest track record, structured limits that build discipline for you, and a proven, reliable payout process.
- **Choose MyFundedFutures** for plan flexibility, no daily loss limit, and fast payouts — provided you bring your own daily stop.

If you're new or benefit from structure, lean Topstep.
