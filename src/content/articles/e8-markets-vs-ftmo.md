E8 Markets and FTMO answer the same question — "what account do you want to trade?" — in opposite ways. E8 hands you the dials and lets you build it; FTMO hands you a proven, fixed structure and a decade of payout history. Which is better depends entirely on whether you value flexibility or predictability.

## Head to head

| Factor | E8 Markets | FTMO |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Founded | 2021 | 2015 |
| Evaluation | Configurable one-step | 1-step / 2-step |
| Drawdown | Intraday trailing (E8 One) | Static (5% / 10%) |
| Split | Configurable to 100% | To 90% |
| Funded rules | Best-day rule (performance) | Per-trade cap (2026) |
| Payout record | Shorter (newer) | Industry's most documented |

## E8: build-your-own flexibility

[E8 Markets'](/reviews/e8-markets-review/) appeal is control — on E8 One you set your drawdown, daily limit and split at checkout, with a headline up to 100%. For a trader who knows exactly what room their edge needs, that's genuinely useful. The catches: E8 One uses [intraday trailing drawdown](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/), the most breach-prone type, and the funded performance stage adds a best-day rule. Flexibility here comes with complexity — it's easy to configure an attractive-looking account that trades against you.

## FTMO: proven and predictable

[FTMO's](/reviews/ftmo-review/) strength is the opposite: a fixed static 5%/10% structure that never trails, and the most documented payout record in the industry, earned through multiple cycles since 2015. You can't tune it, but you always know exactly where your breach levels are — and you're leaning on the strongest reliability track record available. The 2026 per-trade risk cap is the one funded rule to check against a concentrated-risk style.

## The decision

- **Choose E8 Markets** if you want to configure the account to your edge and value a high configurable split, and you'll manage its intraday trailing and funded best-day rule.
- **Choose FTMO** if you want proven reliability, predictable static rules, and the strongest payout record — flexibility be damned.
