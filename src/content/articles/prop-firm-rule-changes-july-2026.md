We track prop firm rule changes monthly so a mid-cycle surprise never breaches your account or defers your payout. Here's everything material we logged in **July 2026**. For the mechanics behind these rules, see [drawdown rules explained](/guides/prop-firm-drawdown-explained/) and [consistency rules explained](/guides/prop-firm-consistency-rules-explained/).

## FTMO: risk cap now strictly enforced

FTMO's **0.5–1% risk-per-trade limit** on funded accounts — introduced earlier in 2026 — is now fully enforced with automated flagging. Traders reporting soft enforcement in Q2 confirm it's now hard. The practical effect is unchanged from our [FTMO review](/reviews/ftmo-review/): concentrated, high-conviction sizing strategies don't fit funded FTMO accounts anymore, which is why "[FTMO alternatives](/compare/ftmo-alternatives/)" spiked as a search this month. If you risk ≤1% per trade, nothing changes for you.

## Apex: the March overhaul has settled

Apex's [March 2026 restructure](/reviews/apex-trader-funding-review/) — one-time pricing, EOD/intraday drawdown choice, MAE removed, 50% consistency — is now three-plus months in, and community payout-time reports have improved versus the transition period in Q1. The trailing drawdown remains the account-killer it always was; the [Topstep vs Apex](/compare/topstep-vs-apex/) decision is unchanged.

## Two mid-tier firms tightened terms

Two mid-tier firms (outside our current top-rated set) quietly **raised payout minimums and lengthened consistency-measurement windows** in July. These are deferral-type changes, not denials — but they're exactly the kind of retroactive tightening that, when it accelerates, precedes trouble. We flagged both in our tracker and are watching payout-processing times. The pattern to watch is covered in [are prop firms legit?](/guides/are-prop-firms-legit/)

## No adverse changes at the stable firms

[FundedNext](/reviews/fundednext-review/), [The5ers](/reviews/the5ers-review/) and [Topstep](/reviews/topstep-review/) logged no adverse rule changes this month. This is the boring, valuable outcome you're paying a slightly higher fee for — rule stability is a feature, and it's weighted directly in our [methodology](/methodology/).

## What to do this month

- **Aggressive sizers on FTMO:** confirm your strategy fits the 1% cap, or evaluate an [alternative](/compare/ftmo-alternatives/).
- **Everyone:** re-read your firm's current payout-eligibility terms before your next request — check minimums and consistency windows against the [payout guide](/guides/prop-firm-payout-methods/).
- **Withdraw every cycle.** Unchanged, always.
